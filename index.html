What it can detect

26 diseases and pests across 14 crops, plus a healthy class for 12 of them (38 classes in total). Orange and squash have disease classes only.

How it works

The modelMobileNetV2 (pre-trained on ImageNet) with a classification head trained on the PlantVillage dataset, using augmented images (random crops, rotations and lighting changes) for robustness.
Real-time, privateThe full model (~9 MB) runs inside your browser with TensorFlow.js. Each prediction averages the photo and its mirror image, taking a fraction of a second.
Best resultsPhotograph a single leaf, filling most of the frame, in even light. The training images are lab photos of individual leaves, so busy field scenes or other plant species are less reliable.
LeafScan is a decision-support tool, not a lab diagnosis. For serious or spreading problems, confirm with a local agricultural extension service or plant pathologist. Dataset: PlantVillage (Hughes & Salathé, 2015).
